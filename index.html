Wi-Fi mouse and keyboard for your Windows PC

Control your computer from your phone or tablet: touchpad, keyboard, apps, files, sound and microphone. Nothing to install on the phone: scan a code and the remote opens in its browser.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 (2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Beta builds aren't code-signed yet: Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"; choose More info, then Run anyway.

Free, no account, no ads. Everything stays on your home network. The app checks for updates and installs them on its own when no device is connected; you can change that in Settings.

How it works

  1. Install Tonttu on the PC. A QR code appears.
  2. Scan it with the camera of your phone or tablet. The remote opens in the browser; the first time, click Allow on the PC.
  3. Optional, on iPhone or iPad: install the certificate from the app's Certificate page, so the browser stops warning about the connection.

What it does

  • Touchpad and keyboard, with buttons that change with what's on screen: media, browser, video, slides.
  • Slides clicker, app switcher, volume and power.
  • Send files to the PC, download files from it.
  • Use the device as a microphone on the PC (needs a free virtual audio cable such as VB-CABLE).
  • Works with windows that run as administrator, like Task Manager and installers.
