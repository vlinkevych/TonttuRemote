Features

Everything you need to control your PC from your phone.

01

Mouse and keyboard

Turn your phone into a wireless touchpad and keyboard for your Windows PC.

  • Wireless touchpad.
  • Mouse cursor control.
  • Clicking, scrolling and dragging.
  • Multitouch gestures.
  • Type on your PC using your phone’s keyboard.

Use your phone as a mouse for PC and as a keyboard, with Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, Win and arrow keys one tap away.

02

Context-aware controls

Convenient controls that adapt to what you’re doing on your PC.

  • Browser controls.
  • Media playback controls.
  • Presentation and slideshow controls.
  • File operations.

The buttons under the touchpad change with the active application. Open a browser, play a video, start a slideshow or select files in File Explorer, and the matching controls appear on your phone.

Phone → PC

Send files from your phone or tablet to your PC

Photos, videos, any file, or the picture on your clipboard: pick several at once and they land on the PC.

  • Tap Send to PC, choose Photos & videos or Files.
  • The phone shows where the files will go: the folder open in File Explorer, or the PC’s received-files folder.
  • If you choose so, the PC asks before saving each file.

Wireless file transfer over your Wi-Fi. Transfer files between Windows and iPad, between PC and Android, and from PC to iPhone, all without a cable.

FAQ

Questions people ask

More in Help →

Can I use my phone as a mouse for my PC?

Yes. Tonttu turns your phone screen into a touchpad for Windows: drag to move the cursor, tap to click, two fingers to scroll or right-click.

Does Tonttu work without an internet connection?

Yes. The phone and the PC talk over your local network, so they only need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

How can I control my Windows PC from my iPhone?

Install Tonttu on the PC, scan the QR code with the iPhone camera and allow the device on the PC. The remote opens in Safari.

Is my connection encrypted?

Yes. The connection uses HTTPS with a certificate your PC creates. That’s why the browser shows a warning the first time.

Can I use my iPad as a mouse and keyboard for Windows?

Yes. The remote works in Safari on iPad, with a landscape layout that keeps the touchpad and keys on screen together.

Does Tonttu work outside my home?

No. The phone and the PC need to be on the same network. Nothing goes over the internet.

Do I need to install an app on my phone?

No. The remote is a web page served by your PC and opens in your phone’s browser.

Does Tonttu support Android?

Yes. The remote opens in Chrome on Android phones and tablets, with the same touchpad, keyboard and controls.

Can I transfer files from PC to iPhone?

Yes. Select a file in File Explorer on the PC, then tap Download on the phone. It’s saved to Files › Downloads.

Can I transfer files between PC and Android?

Yes, both ways. Send to PC uploads from the phone, and Download saves files from the PC to the phone’s Downloads.

Can I transfer files from iPhone to PC?

Yes. Tap Send to PC and pick photos, videos or files. They land in the folder open on the PC, or in its received-files folder.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit.

Can I download folders from my PC to my phone?

Yes. Folders and multiple files are sent as one zip file, which the Files app opens with a tap.

Is Tonttu free?

Yes. It’s free, no ads, no user account.

Can I transfer files between Windows and iPad?

Yes, in both directions, the same way as on iPhone.

Can I control multiple PCs?

Install Tonttu on each PC. Each one shows its own QR code, and the remote shows the name of the PC it’s connected to.

Can I use Tonttu without Bluetooth?

Yes. Tonttu uses Wi-Fi, not Bluetooth, so there is nothing to pair in Bluetooth settings.
